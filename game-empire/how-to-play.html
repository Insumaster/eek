How to play

The same guide as under ? in the game.

The goal

Start in a garage in 1980 and build a game empire before the year 2000. Make good games, sell lots of copies, grow your team and reach the milestones. In 2000 your studio gets a final score for the Hall of Fame. Keep playing, and in 2026 there is a second Hall of Fame.

Time

Time only runs on the Studio screen. Use the buttons at the top: pause, normal, fast and very fast. One week is about a second.

Making a game

Tap Develop new game. Pick a topic, a genre, a console and a size. Some topics and genres fit together much better than others - reviews tell you which.

The three phases

Each game has three phases with three parts each. Split the team's time with the + and - buttons. Each genre cares about different parts: an RPG needs story, an Action game needs graphics and engine. Reviews tell you what mattered.

Design and tech

Your team makes design points (blue) and tech points (orange) - the code on their screens shows what they work on. Each genre wants its own mix. Red lines are bugs - fix them before release.

Cover

When the game is done you design the box cover. A picture that fits the topic and a title that is easy to read sell more copies.

Release and reviews

Release when you are happy with the bugs. Four magazines review the game. The bar rises every year and with your own best game, so you have to keep getting better.

Marketing and hype

Marketing builds hype, and hype sells more copies in the first weeks. But if a hyped game is bad, fans get angry.

Charts and sequels

The top 10 shows the best-selling games every week. A hit can get a sequel, but it must be better than the original - and players get tired of long series.

Ports and remasters

For five years after release you can port a game to other consoles from its game page - it keeps its score and sells about half as well. After five years the ports stop and you can make one remaster instead: half the work, nostalgia hype, and better reviews if you have new technology since the original.

Your team

Move to an office to hire people. Watch their energy: tired people work slower and make more bugs. Send them on vacation, and send them on courses to get better.

Traits, leads and happiness

Everyone you hire has a trait - a creative mind, a workhorse, a perfectionist. People with Design or Tech 60+ can become Lead Designer or Lead Programmer and lift the whole team. Keep people rested and paid what they are worth: after three unhappy months they may quit.

Team 2

In the HQ Tower you can split the staff into two teams. Team 2 makes its own Small, Medium or Large games while you work on yours - it uses what your reviews have taught you.

Studios and accessories

Garage, office, big office and from 1995 the HQ Tower. Tap the arrows on the studio picture to see the side rooms. Under Staff you can buy office accessories - coffee, plants, arcade machines, a rooftop pool - that make the team less tired, faster or more creative. They move along for free when you move, but each studio only has so many places.

Events

Sometimes something happens during development. Choose wisely - there is no right answer for every studio.

Research

Your team earns research points. Spend them on new topics and technology. With enough money you can build your own engine - and later even your own console.

Your own consoles

Research the Hardware Lab and move to the big office to build a console of your own. Your first one is Basic; better ones come years later. Tap a console to see every game on it. Ten years after launch you can release a Mini of it with all its games built in - ports and remasters too.

Money

Salaries and rent are paid every month, and rent goes up over the years. If money drops below -$50,000 the studio goes bankrupt - but the first time the bank offers a rescue loan. Under Business you can borrow money. From 1987 a publisher can pay for development, but keeps half the sales.

Business

Book a stand at PixelCon every June for fans and hype. From 1997 your best games can get an esports league with sponsors. From 1990 in the big office you can publish games made by small studios - pay an advance and keep half of every sale.

Game of the Year

Every December your best game of the year meets the rivals' best at the gala. Win and it sells 50% more, and new fans pour in. Later in the game more studios compete, so each game sells a little less.

Saving

The game saves every time you tap, and every game month.
