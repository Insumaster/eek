The game

It is 1980. You have a computer, a garage and a big dream. Make your first game, read the reviews, learn what players want - and grow your tiny studio into the biggest name in games.

  • Pick a topic and a genre, split your team's time and design your own box cover.
  • Four magazines review every game, and the bar rises every year.
  • Move from the garage to an office, a big office and your own HQ Tower, and hire a team with their own traits.
  • Make sequels, ports and remasters, climb the top 10 and win Game of the Year.
  • Build your own consoles, book PixelCon stands, start esports leagues and publish other studios' games.
  • Reach the year 2000 for the Hall of Fame - then keep playing to 2026.

New in beta 0.13

  • Sound and music! Chiptune sounds and a little tune - turn them on or off under ?.
  • Business (studio): borrow money from the bank, book a stand at PixelCon every June, start esports leagues for your best games (1997+), and publish games made by small studios (1990+).
  • Game of the Year gala every December: win and your game sells 50% more.
  • Your staff have traits - creative, workhorse, perfectionist and more. Make your best people Lead Designer or Lead Programmer.
  • Tired or underpaid people get unhappy, and after a while they may quit. Give them a raise in time!
  • Team 2: in the HQ Tower a second team makes its own games while you work on yours.
  • Pop-up notes for the big moments: number 1 on the charts, console launches, prizes and more.
  • A warning when the money runs low, and the bank saves a bankrupt studio once with a rescue loan.
  • The late game is harder: rents and big games cost more over the years, and more studios compete for players.
  • Statistics show what each of your consoles has earned. Consoles are now found under Business.

Fair to play
