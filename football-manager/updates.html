Updates

Every version of Pixel Football Manager, newest first.

beta 0.41

2026-10-27
  • Women's football: choose Men or Women for Sweden and Norway - Damallsvenskan, Elitettan, Division 1 and 2 Dam, Toppserien and 1., 2. and 3. divisjon kvinner, with the same cups as the men
  • The top two divisions are a step up: 50% bigger crowds, but 40% higher wages, staff wages and running costs for the ground
  • Shops at the top: fan club members above 3 000 buy less in the club shop, and food and drink sell a little less in the top division
  • The Scandinavian Cup: a cup for every level, where the best 8 clubs of the level in Sweden and in Norway meet. Four groups of four, everyone plays everyone once, the top two go on to the quarter-finals. Every match pays like a cup match, and the winner gets the cup prize

beta 0.40

2026-10-25
  • Norway: step 1 is now choosing the country. Play in Sweden or in Norway - Eliteserien, 1., 2. and 3. divisjon, 64 Norwegian clubs on the map, Norwegian players and the Super Norge Cup
  • The start page shows the flags of Sweden and Norway
  • The board sets a target every season - promotion, top 3, top 6, top half or staying up. Meet it for a bonus and fan support; miss it three seasons in a row and you are sacked. The board's confidence is on the Manager page
  • Player morale: happy players play +1 or +2, upset ones -2. Good players left on the bench get upset, and under 20 they ask to leave. A coloured square in the squad shows the mood
  • Season awards: Golden Boot, Player of the Year and Young Player of the Year in the league and in your team, in the Christmas paper and the History
  • Win bonus: with 8 league rounds left and your club in the top 6, pay one or two weeks' wages per win for +1 or +2 in every match of the run-in
  • Youth: a group trains for 52 weeks from the day it joins, then you choose who joins the first team and a new group starts
  • At the top: growth above a rating of 80 is harder, stars cost more in wages, and when you run away with the top league the rivals sign new stars and bid big for your best players

beta 0.39

2026-10-24
  • Penalty shoot-out: a level cup or playoff match goes to penalties. Choose Simple (your best five shoot and you watch, with fast forward) or Interactive (pick the takers, choose where to shoot and where your keeper dives)
  • Tactics has its own page: your team on the pitch seen from above, with formation, mentality, playing style and pressing
  • Roofs come in four steps per stand: a wooden roof (18 000) before level 5, a big wooden roof (100 000) before level 9, a modern roof (500 000) before level 13 and an arena roof (1 200 000) before the corners and the hotel. Expanding a roofed stand no longer costs 30% more
  • The fan club grows towards a quarter of your home crowd - faster when the fans love you. Promotion brings 20% more members, a cup win 10%
  • Academy signings stay at the club for two seasons: they cannot be sold or released before then
  • Finances: last season's profit or loss is shown as "From last season" just above NET
  • A player who decides to retire gives a message with a button to his page, and a red (R) in the squad list
  • Top 5 Scorers in The Pixel Post also shows your own best scorer in red when your club is not in the top five
  • Transfers: every tab has a history of old bids, offers and sales
  • News keeps the latest 100 news lines and 300 newspapers
  • Messages: old ones disappear when a newer one about the same thing comes, and the box scrolls when there are many
  • Long club names fit on the club home
  • The start page shows the flags of Sweden, Denmark, Norway, Finland and Estonia as shields, and has a ? button next to Exit

beta 0.37

2026-10-22
  • Fans follow the table: a top-3 finish gives +10%, the bottom three -15%, and wins only lift support up to 80% - so a losing manager can get the sack
  • Promoted clubs are stronger: +6 to every attribute and a promotion bonus of 100 000 euro from the sponsors
  • A popup 10 weeks before contracts end, with a button to the squad; the free players you pick when under 15 are at your squad's level
  • New map when you choose your club: the Nordic countries in square tiles of green, with Estonia and Latvia too; every club is a square - red Allsvenskan, white Superettan, purple Ettan, yellow Division 2 - and the chosen club gets a white frame. The map no longer jumps when you pick a club
  • Medium now starts with 250 000 euro (Hard 50 000, Easy 500 000)
  • Players over 30 get worse a little faster, and half the stamina won in matches goes over the summer, so the leagues stay steadier
  • Finances: sponsors pay every week, so each sponsor row shows paid so far of the whole deal; the start support sponsor is shown under the shirt sponsor
  • Transfers: the market compares a player with your best and your weakest in the same position
  • Offers say if the player is on your transfer list or not, and with an assistant you get advice: keep him or sell
  • The manager page has two tabs: Manager and Staff
  • Updates show the date of every version
  • How to play: the game is fully offline and needs no internet or mobile data once downloaded

  • Weather on the pictures: rain on the stadium, the club home and the training ground when it rains that week, and falling snow when it snows
  • New way to choose your club: a drop-down list with town and club name, and a pixel map of Sweden with a dot for every club from Allsvenskan to Division 2 - tap a dot to pick that club
