Updates

Every version of Pixel Football Manager, newest first.

beta 0.37

2026-10-22
  • Fans follow the table: a top-3 finish gives +10%, the bottom three -15%, and wins only lift support up to 80% - so a losing manager can get the sack
  • Promoted clubs are stronger: +6 to every attribute and a promotion bonus of 100 000 euro from the sponsors
  • A popup 10 weeks before contracts end, with a button to the squad; the free players you pick when under 15 are at your squad's level
  • New map when you choose your club: the Nordic countries in square tiles of green, with Estonia and Latvia too; every club is a square - red Allsvenskan, white Superettan, purple Ettan, yellow Division 2 - and the chosen club gets a white frame. The map no longer jumps when you pick a club
  • Medium now starts with 250 000 euro (Hard 50 000, Easy 500 000)
  • Players over 30 get worse a little faster, and half the stamina won in matches goes over the summer, so the leagues stay steadier
  • Finances: sponsors pay every week, so each sponsor row shows paid so far of the whole deal; the start support sponsor is shown under the shirt sponsor
  • Transfers: the market compares a player with your best and your weakest in the same position
  • Offers say if the player is on your transfer list or not, and with an assistant you get advice: keep him or sell
  • The manager page has two tabs: Manager and Staff
  • Updates show the date of every version
  • How to play: the game is fully offline and needs no internet or mobile data once downloaded

beta 0.36

2026-10-20
  • Fairer start: your squad starts just one point under the Division 2 average
  • Shops, food and bar are cheaper to run (15 euro per level a week)
  • Fans: losses cost at most 10% a season and support comes back 10% towards 50% each new season; under 20% the board warns you, two seasons under 10% and you are sacked
  • Fighter bonus: clubs at the bottom get 10% better sponsor offers, the champion a little less
  • Slower rating growth and older players get worse, so the leagues do not get better and better
  • Grass in Division 2 wears 2 every third match; a basic youth coach costs 100 euro a week
  • At most 3 youngsters can join the first team each season
  • The ! test-money button is gone
  • Building takes longer again: each stand's expansions take 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and then 6 weeks; roofs, shops, screen, corners, hotel and the training ground take 6 weeks
  • How to play under ?: a section with a picture, steps and tips for every part of the game, plus Tips & tricks and the privacy policy
  • Weather on the pictures: rain on the stadium, the club home and the training ground when it rains that week, and falling snow when it snows
  • New way to choose your club: a drop-down list with town and club name, and a pixel map of Sweden with a dot for every club from Allsvenskan to Division 2 - tap a dot to pick that club

beta 0.35

2026-10-19
  • Running costs: the stadium costs 0.25 euro per seat a week (plus roofs, LED screen and shops), the training ground from 100 up to 4 800 euro a week - shown in Finances, the finance report and Facilities
  • Popups on the club home when the transfer window opens, closes in a week and has closed, and when bids for your players come in
  • Fewer than 15 players: you first pick from five free players (a little below your squad) until you have 15
  • Finances: the start money is shown as a support sponsor, grass gets its own row, and the net result is bigger at the bottom
  • New cup: the Super Svenskan Cup for every club in Allsvenskan and Superettan, same money as the Crown Cup
  • Tables has two buttons, Leagues and Cup; under Cup the arrows switch between the Crown Cup, Challenge Cup, Super Svenskan Cup and the promotion playoffs
  • Difficulty has its own page at the end of a new career, with a kid (Easy), an ordinary man (Medium) and a tough guy (Hard)
  • Stadium is now Facilities: two buttons at the top switch between Stadium and Training (the youth ground, moved from Squad)
  • Smaller Save & Exit button with the ! button between ? and Save & Exit
  • New staff: Janitor in three levels cuts the running costs by 10, 20 or 30%; a good janitor makes the grass last longer, a top one also keeps the bar, food outlet and shop open while they are rebuilt

beta 0.34

2026-10-19
  • New look for the stadium and the youth ground: countryside with fields, a ditch, a road, trees, cows and a car park that fills up as the ground grows
  • Small grounds get red tin roofs; when you build the corners the whole ground gets a white membrane roof
  • Corners are wedges that fit between the stands, under one joined roof
  • Step 5 after the corners is now a glass hotel with an H on the roof (the office towers are gone)
  • Merch shop next to the entrance; bar, food and merch are all turned with their windows towards you and get a sign on the roof when they are big enough
  • Youth ground drawn like the stadium, with houses with tiled roofs, a running track, sports hall, seats, floodlights and a fence
  • Youth is now Youth & Training: the first team trains at the youth ground. Every step gives something - faster recovery, +passing, +power, +stamina, fewer injuries, quicker rehab, more fan support - and up to two more youngsters
  • A town behind the North End and East Stand that matches the club's home town: village houses, a community with a church, a smaller town with shops and flats, or a big city with tall buildings
  • Parks with paths, benches and a statue of a footballer in front of the ground and beyond the road
