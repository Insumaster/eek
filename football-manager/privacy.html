Pixel Football Manager - last updated 2026-10-22.

Privacy policy

Pixel Football Manager is a game you play on your own phone. This page says what happens with your data.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, send or sell any personal data. There is no account, no login, no ads and no tracking.

Your saves

Your careers are saved only on your phone, in the game's own storage. They are removed if you uninstall the game.

Internet

The game is fully offline. Once it is downloaded it needs no internet and no mobile data, and it does not talk to any server.

Purchases

There are no in-app purchases. If a one-time Plus purchase comes later, it is handled by Google Play and the game only checks whether you own it.

Children

The game does not ask for any personal information and can be played by everyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the game under ? and on the store page.

Contact

Questions? Use the contact e-mail on the game's Google Play page.
