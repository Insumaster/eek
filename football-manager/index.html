The game

Play in Sweden or Norway. Pick any club, from Allsvenskan or Eliteserien down to the fourth level, and start at the very bottom of the pyramid: one wooden stand, a small budget and a squad that needs you. Can you take them all the way to the top?

  • Watch every match live on a pixel pitch and change tactics and players during the game.
  • Build your ground stand by stand, from 150 seats to a huge arena with roofs, shops, a bar and a hotel.
  • Grow your own stars at the youth ground, where your first team trains too.
  • Scout, bid and haggle, sell players, hire staff, take loans and keep the fans and the board happy.
  • Four leagues, promotion playoffs and three cups - with rain and snow through the seasons.
  • Your assistant helps you through the first two seasons, and the board remembers what you have won.
  • Coming later: the game grows country by country to all of Europe - 32 countries in 8 regions, each with 4 leagues for men and 4 for women.

New in beta 0.46

  • Your assistant gives tips in your first two seasons: a card the first time you open the club home, Facilities, Squad, a match, Transfers, Finances, Manager and Training, and when the first transfer window, injury and derby come. Got it or Hide all tips - and ? > How to play shows them again
  • What's new: the first start after an update shows the most important news, with a button to every update
  • Success costs: in the top division the players want a share of what the club earns (about 55%). The club's wage level rises up to 18% a season - never after a season that lost money - new contracts follow it, and it falls back when the income drops. Shown under Finances
  • Stands and the hotel wear 8% a season once they are built. A worn stand sells fewer tickets and an unsafe one is half closed. Repair it under Facilities, or Modernise it for VIP boxes (+10% ticket money). Tested over 30 seasons: a top club ends up with about 150-300 million instead of 600
  • The board remembers: a top-division title, a big cup or a promotion earns trust, up to 3. A missed target uses one trust instead of counting towards the sack - shown as three cups on the Manager page
  • The difficulty page says what really changes: the start money, the rivals at the top and champions' pay
  • Smoother on cheaper phones: the game saves at most every few seconds instead of on every tap - every 2 seconds on a fast phone, up to 20 on a slow one. A new week, leaving a match, Save & Exit and leaving the app still save at once
  • Manager page: fan support, team power and the board's confidence are shown as bars of the same size. Team power also shows the league average
  • Captain & set pieces has moved to the Squad page, right under Tactics. Every player's power is shown on his own, the buttons are smaller, and an i button opens the player page
  • Facilities: the merch shop, food and bar show what they earn now - per home match and so far this season - and what the next step would earn
  • Supporters: the home end can only be moved to the North or South End, behind the goals
  • Badges added in a new version are given for what your career has already done, with the season you earned them
  • Fixtures: Last rounds shows the three latest rounds

Fair to play

  • No pay-to-win: you can never pay to get a better team - everyone plays on the same level.
  • Fully offline: no internet and no mobile data needed once it is downloaded.
  • Saves on your phone - three save slots.
  • Easy, Medium or Hard: the start money, the rivals at the top and the champions' pay change.
