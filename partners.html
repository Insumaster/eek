Ways to work together

Cars & EVs

Test drives, first impressions, 0-100 tests and long road trips. We test on real Swedish roads - in summer heat and in winter at -10 °C - and show range, charging and everyday life as it really is.

Tech & gadgets

Chargers, EV accessories, travel tech and the latest gadgets: unboxed, used for real and reviewed with honest impressions.

Events & trips

Car shows, EV expos, launches and press events, or a road trip through Europe with your car or product along for the ride.

Games & apps

Fellow indie developers, publishers and testers: let's talk about collaborations, cross-promotion or helping each other make better games and apps.

How we work

  • Honest, always. Our viewers trust our impressions, and we keep it that way - also in sponsored videos. You get our real opinion, the good and the bad.
  • Clearly marked. Sponsored content and products we got to borrow or keep are always marked as advertising, as Swedish law requires.
  • A good fit. We only say yes to things we would be interested in ourselves, and that our viewers will enjoy.
  • Fair games. Sponsors never change how our games play. Everyone plays on the same level, with no pay-to-win.
  • Clean visuals. We care about picture and sound in every video we make.
