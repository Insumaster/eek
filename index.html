About us

EEK is a small independent maker from Sweden. We make games, apps and videos we enjoy ourselves: simple to start, deep enough to keep you going, and fair. That means:

  • No pay-to-win - you can never pay to get better, everyone plays on the same level.
  • Fully offline - once a game or app is downloaded it needs no internet and no mobile data.
  • No accounts, no tracking - your saves and data stay on your phone.

Support us

If you enjoy what we make and want to help us make more, you can buy us a coffee. It is completely voluntary and you get nothing extra in the games: everyone plays on the same level.
