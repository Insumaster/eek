About us

Pixel is a small independent game maker from Sweden. We make the kind of games we want to play ourselves: simple to start, deep enough to keep you going for many seasons, and fair. That means:

  • No ads and no in-app purchases - you never pay to win.
  • Fully offline - once a game is downloaded it needs no internet and no mobile data.
  • No accounts, no tracking - your saves stay on your phone.

Support us

Our games have no ads and no in-app purchases - and they never will. If you enjoy them and want to help us make more, you can buy us a coffee. It is completely voluntary and you get nothing extra in the games: everyone plays the same game.
