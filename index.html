About us

EEK is the two of us - a small independent team from Sweden. It started with a love for cars, technology and games - and a habit of making the things we want to see ourselves. Today EEK is three things:

  • Eek On Road - our YouTube channel since 2024. Road trips across Europe, electric cars tested on real Swedish roads in summer and winter, car shows, events and the latest gadgets.
  • Pixel Games - management and tycoon games in pixel style for your phone. Simple to start, deep enough to keep you going for a long time, and always fair.
  • Pixel Apps - small, useful apps for everyday things. The first one is on its way.

Whatever we make, we keep it honest: videos with clean visuals and our real impressions - the good and the bad - games where everyone plays on the same level, and apps that do what they say.
